# 100 &bull; TYSON Media Group

> **100 Subscribers Milestone Celebration Platform**  
> *Stillness in Motion &bull; Ad Astra Per Aspera*

A minimalist, vector-traced typographic intro experience commemorating **100 Subscribers** on YouTube for **TYSON Media Group**.

---

## 🎬 Animation Choreography

1. **Stage 1 — "100"**:
   - **Font**: [Borg 9](https://www.dafont.com/borg9.font) by Raymond Larabie / Typodermic Fonts (CC0 Public Domain).
   - **Action**: Traces out geometric vector outlines letter-by-letter, fills in solid white with ambient glow, then smoothly fades out.

2. **Stage 2 — "2,199 days"**:
   - **Font**: Cormorant Garamond (*italic*).
   - **Action**: Calligraphy pen-traces each glyph sequentially across all 10 characters, fills in, holds, and fades out.

3. **Stage 3 — "80 videos"**:
   - **Font**: Cormorant Garamond (*italic*).
   - **Action**: Vector trace across characters, fills in, holds, and fades out.

4. **Stage 4 — "14,140 views"**:
   - **Font**: Cormorant Garamond (*italic*).
   - **Action**: Vector trace across characters, fills in, holds, and **slowly fades out** into cinematic silence.

5. **Stage 5 — Finale Crossfade**:
   - **Font**: Cormorant Garamond (*italic*).
   - **Action**: Gently fades in **"100 Subscribers"**, holds briefly, then crossfades seamlessly into **"Thank You"** with an ethereal ambient glow.
   - Reveals the official TYSON Media Group footer badge, link to `rpm.tysonmediagroup.org`, and interactive controls.

---

## ⌨️ Controls & Interactions

- **Replay**: Click the **Replay** button in the bottom dock or press <kbd>Space</kbd> / <kbd>R</kbd> at any time.
- **Sound**: Optional ambient audio accompaniment toggle in the bottom dock.
- **Responsive**: Fully vector-rendered via SVG, perfectly scaling from mobile displays to 4K Ultrawide monitors.
